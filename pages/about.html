Explore Our Menu
OUR STORY

Made for the campus community

CPCC Bites started with a simple idea: students should be able to enjoy a great meal without spending too much time or money.

Whether you are grabbing lunch between classes, meeting friends after school, or looking for a quick meal to take with you, CPCC Bites is here to make your day a little better.

Our menu brings together classic favorites, fresh ingredients, and affordable student options in a welcoming campus environment.

WHAT WE VALUE

More than just a meal

CPCC Bites is about creating a place where students can eat, relax, connect, and enjoy their campus experience.

  • Affordable meals for students
  • Friendly and welcoming service
  • Fresh and delicious food
  • Convenient ordering options
